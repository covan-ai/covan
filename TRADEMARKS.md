# Trademarks

The [licence](LICENSE) gives you rights in Covan's code. It gives you no rights
in its name or its logo: the Functional Source License says so itself, under
"Trademarks". This page says what you may do with
them anyway, so that nobody has to ask for the ordinary cases.

"Covan" and the Covan logo are the marks of the project's maintainer,
Mahmut Efe Dara. They are how somebody tells the software made here from
software made elsewhere, and that is the only thing this policy protects.

## Fine without asking

- Saying what your software is built on: "based on Covan", "a fork of Covan",
  "built with Covan", "compatible with Covan".
- Writing about Covan, reviewing it, teaching it, comparing it, linking to it.
- Running an unmodified build for your own team and calling it Covan inside
  your organisation.
- Using the name in a talk, a blog post or a bug report.
- Showing the logo, unaltered, next to text that is about this project.

## Ask first

- Calling a modified version "Covan", or a name that could be read as Covan
  ("Covan Pro", "OpenCovan", "Covan for Teams").
- Using the name or logo for a hosted service, a product, a company, a domain,
  or an app-store listing.
- Merchandise, or anything that suggests the project endorses you.

## If you fork

Rename it. Replace the logo and the product name in the interface, the page
titles and the emails it sends, and keep a line that says it is based on
Covan. The copyright notices in the code stay where they are; the licence
requires that, and it is a different thing from the name.

## Asking

Write to efe@covan.app with what you would like to do. Most requests are a
quick yes.
